VideoCrop: design concepts

Five takes on the VideoCrop site for the Vexbyte suite. Open one, pick a favourite.

  1. 01 · TIMELINE

    Cut to the good part

    An interactive editing timeline is the hero: trim handles close in and the playhead scrubs under your cursor.

  2. 02 · CROP FRAME

    Frame it right

    The icon's amber crop brackets morph between 16:9, 1:1, 9:16 and more around the headline.

  3. 03 · SQUEEZE

    Make it fit

    A 1.84 GB clip compresses live into a Discord-ready 9.7 MB, then cycles through every share target.

  4. 04 · CINEMA

    Your footage, finished

    Letterbox reveal, film grain, sprocket strips and a running timecode. Filmic but modern.

  5. 05 · SIGNAL

    Plays anything. Instantly.

    A field of keyframes and streamed segments: the technical story behind instant playback of huge files.
