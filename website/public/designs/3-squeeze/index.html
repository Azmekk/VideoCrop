How it fits

A size cap, turned into a plan.

You pick where the clip is going. VideoCrop works out how many bits that leaves, picks a resolution that still looks good at that rate, then encodes twice so the file lands just under the line.

1Budget the bitrate

The cap divided by the clip's length is the bitrate you can afford. Audio takes its share first.

10 MB ÷ 3:12 = 437 kb/s
videoaudio · margin

2Walk down the ladder

Too few bits for 1080p means mush. It steps down until the picture holds up, and never upscales.

1080p✕
720p✕
540p✓
360p

3Encode in two passes

Pass one reads the whole clip. Pass two spends bits on the busy fights and saves them on the menus.

The rest of the kit

Trim it, frame it, then send it.

  • Cut

    A zoomable timeline with a filmstrip. Drag away from the track to scrub slower, or type exact times. Choose frame-accurate or an instant keyframe cut.

  • Crop

    Frame it on a full-resolution still with a live preview. 16:9, 4:3, 1:1, 9:16, 4:5 or the original aspect.

  • Resize

    Aspect lock, 75, 50 and 25% options, and height presets that never upscale.

  • Plays everything

    Multi-hour OBS recordings in HEVC, AV1 or MKV open instantly. Files Windows can't decode stream through a GPU preview. The export is always made from the original.

  • Private

    Runs locally with ffmpeg. No telemetry, no uploads. It uses your ffmpeg if one is on your PATH, or downloads one once.

  • Light or dark

    Both themes, a 5 MB app that opens instantly, and a popup when a new version is out.
