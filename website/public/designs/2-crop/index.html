Under the hood

Playback and privacy

Plays the files Windows won't

Multi-hour OBS recordings in HEVC, AV1 or MKV open instantly. When Windows can't decode a file, VideoCrop streams a preview instead, on the GPU when there is one. The export is always made from the original.

HEVCAV1MKVMP4MOV

Your clips stay on your PC

Everything runs locally with ffmpeg. There's no telemetry and nothing is uploaded. If ffmpeg is already on your PATH, VideoCrop uses it. If not, it downloads one, once.
