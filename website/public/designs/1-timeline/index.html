Cut

Trim to the frame, or to the keyframe.

Zoom the timeline until each frame of the filmstrip is visible, then drag the handles or type the times.

  • ZoomScroll to zoom the filmstrip, Shift+scroll to pan, or jump straight to the cut.
  • Fine scrubDrag away from the track and the playhead slows down, so you can land on a single frame.
  • Typed timesEnter a start and end like 00:01.500, or set them from the playhead with one click.
  • Two modesAccurate re-encodes from the exact frame. Fast copies from the nearest keyframe, in seconds.

Private

Your video never leaves your PC.

Everything runs locally with ffmpeg. There's no account, no telemetry and no upload step.

Your fileRead from your disk
ffmpegEncodes on this PC
Your folderSaved next to the original

Uses your ffmpeg

If ffmpeg is already on your PATH, VideoCrop uses it. Otherwise it downloads one, once.

No telemetry

It only goes online to check for a new version, and to fetch ffmpeg once if you don’t already have it.
